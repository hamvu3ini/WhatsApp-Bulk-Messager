WhatsApp Multi-Sender Utility is a standalone Windows 10/11 desktop application, built with Python and PySide6, that sends personalised messages to people you know through your own linked WhatsApp account. Link WhatsApp once by scanning a QR code, load recipients from Excel/CSV or add them by hand, and personalise each message with tags like {Name}, {Var1} and {Var2}. You can attach a photo, video or document to every message. Messages go out one by one with random safety delays, live progress, pause/resume/stop controls and a CSV delivery report.

The app runs entirely on your computer, protected by a master password, and ships as an offline installer with everything bundled.
